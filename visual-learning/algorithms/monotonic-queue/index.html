⚙️ Deque Monotonicity Rules
  1. Monotonic Invariant: Keep the Deque strictly sorted in decreasing order: V[front] > V[second] > ... > V[back].
  2. Ejecting: When a new value $X$ arrives, pop elements from the back of the Deque that are $\le X$. They can never become a future maximum.
  3. Pushing: Once all smaller elements are ejected, push $X$'s index onto the back.
  4. Expiration: If the front element's index is out of bounds (index < Left_Window), pop/expire it from the front of the Deque.
  5. Querying Maximum: The absolute maximum value of the sliding window is always found instantly at the front of the Deque.
